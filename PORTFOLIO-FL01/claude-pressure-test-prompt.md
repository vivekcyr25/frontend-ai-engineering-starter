# Claude Sitemap Pressure-Test Prompt

Run this exact prompt inside your configured Claude Project (`Vivek — AI Engineering Portfolio`):

---

```text
I have designed this initial portfolio sitemap:

HOME
├── WORK / CASE STUDIES
├── ABOUT
└── CONTACT

My proof statement is:

"I build AI-assisted software and practical engineering projects that turn real problems into working solutions."

My primary visitor action is:

"View my projects / case studies."

Act as my critical portfolio tutor.

Pressure-test this sitemap against:

1. My proof statement
2. My primary visitor action
3. The visitor journey from first landing to believing my claim
4. Whether every page genuinely earns its place
5. Whether any page should be removed, merged, or renamed
6. Whether any important information is missing
7. Whether the structure gives enough space to prove my claim through real project evidence

Do not redesign the portfolio unnecessarily.

Give me:

- What works
- What is weak
- Specific changes you recommend
- The final sitemap you recommend
- A short explanation of why the revised sitemap better supports my claim and primary action

Be critical rather than simply agreeing with my current structure.
```
