## 4. Rejected Alternatives

1. **Rejected Claim 1:** *"I build cutting-edge AI and Machine Learning solutions."*
   - **Why Rejected:** Far too broad and unprovable. It claims generalized AI research expertise rather than the specific software engineering skill of chaining, debugging, and optimizing existing models in a pipeline.

2. **Rejected Claim 2:** *"I write performance-optimized Python automation scripts."*
   - **Why Rejected:** Too generic. Any computer science student can write basic automation scripts; it completely ignores the complex multimedia synchronization, computer vision integration, and domain-specific artifact debugging demonstrated in the project.

3. **Rejected Claim 3:** *"Full-Stack & AI Software Developer for companies and startups."*
   - **Why Rejected:** Dilutes the value proposition across too many disciplines (frontend, backend, AI) and targets a vague, undefined audience ("companies") with no single conversion path.
