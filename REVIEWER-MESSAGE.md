# Message to Send Your Reviewer

Copy the message below and send it to a real friend, fellow student, or classmate.

---

## Message to Copy

> Hey — can you do me a quick favour? I need a real first impression of my portfolio for an internship assignment.
>
> Please open this link:
> **[INSERT YOUR LIVE PORTFOLIO URL HERE]**
>
> Just look at it for 10 seconds — don't dig deep — then answer these two questions honestly:
>
> **1. In 10 seconds, what do you think I do?**
>
> **2. Would you believe I'm good at it? Why or why not?**
>
> After that, tell me anything that felt confusing, weak, broken, unnecessary, or difficult to understand.
>
> Please be honest. Don't worry about offending me — I won't defend the design. I actually need your first impression, not encouragement.
>
> You don't need to know anything about coding or tech. Your reaction as a normal person is exactly what I need.

---

## What to Ask Them to Send Back

Ask them to answer all of these (even short answers are fine):

1. In 10 seconds, what do you think I do?
2. Would you believe I'm good at it? Why or why not?
3. Was it clear where to go after the homepage?
4. After opening a case study, did you understand what engineering problem I solved?
5. Was anything confusing, broken, hard to read, or unnecessary?
6. Did you check it on your phone? What was your first reaction? *(optional)*
7. Anything else that felt off?

---

## Important Reminders

- Send this to **at least one real person** (ideally 2–3 for better signal).
- The reviewer does **not** need to be technical.
- Do **not** coach them before they answer — let their first impression be genuine.
- Copy their exact words when filling in the review form.
